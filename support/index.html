How does Nini predict my period?

Nini averages your recent cycle lengths (recent cycles weigh more), estimates ovulation 14 days before the next period and shows a fertile window from 5 days before to 1 day after ovulation. Predictions run on your phone and improve after 2–3 logged cycles. Nini is not a contraceptive and not medical advice.

How do I turn on backup?

You → Data & privacy → Encrypted backup. Write down the 12-word recovery phrase; without it a backup cannot be restored by anyone, including Baem Tech.

How do I delete everything?

You → Data & privacy → Delete everything. This is immediate on the device and removes any backup within 30 days.

Can I change my companion?

Yes: You → Companion. Nini the rabbit is the default; seven other companions are available and the choice is saved on your device.
