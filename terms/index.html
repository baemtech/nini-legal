Subscription information

Updated 21 September 2026 · Baem Tech

Nini Plus is available in monthly and yearly auto-renewing plans. Both remove in-app banner advertising while Plus is active and unlock the Plus features listed in the app. The store displays the localized price, billing period and eligibility for any free trial or introductory offer before you confirm a purchase. Do not assume a trial is available unless the store shows it for your account.

Payment is charged to your Apple or Google account. Auto-renewal continues unless you cancel through that store before its renewal deadline. Cancelling stops future renewals; access normally continues for the paid period, subject to store rules and refunds. Uninstalling Nini or deleting a Nini account does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions and request refunds through the store that charged you.

Use Restore purchases in the app if Plus is missing, with the store account used for purchase. Contact techbaem@gmail.com for support; do not send payment card numbers or medical records.
