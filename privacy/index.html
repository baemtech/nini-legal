Nini Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026 · Baem Tech · techbaem@gmail.com

Nini is a period and cycle tracker for iOS and Android made by Baem Tech. Nini is built so that your health data stays on your phone. This page explains exactly what Nini stores, where, and what it never does.

What data does Nini collect?

Nini stores only what you enter in the app: period days and flow, symptoms and their severity, moods, notes, water intake, optional measurements (weight, sleep, temperature), your goal (period tracking, conception, pregnancy, perimenopause), cycle length answer, companion choice and reminder settings. Nini does not require an account, name, email, phone number or location.

Where is the data stored?

All Nini data is stored in an encrypted database on your device. The encryption key is generated on your phone and kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. Baem Tech has no copy of your data and no key to read it.

Does Nini upload my data?

Not by default. Backup is off unless you turn it on. If you enable encrypted backup, Nini encrypts your data on your phone before uploading it; the server (a MongoDB database operated by Baem Tech) stores only an anonymous identifier and encrypted blobs. Baem Tech cannot decrypt backups. If you lose your recovery phrase, neither can you.

Does Nini use ads, analytics or trackers?

No. Nini contains no advertising, no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics and no tracking pixels. Nini never sells or shares health data with data brokers, advertisers or social networks.

Apple Health and Health Connect

If you allow it, Nini reads cycle data from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) to import your history, and can write period days back so you are never locked in. Health data read by Nini is stored only on your device and is used only to show and predict your cycle. Nini does not use Health data for advertising or share it with third parties.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Notification text is generic by choice and does not reveal health details on the lock screen.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: You → Data & privacy → Delete everything. This removes the on-device database, the encryption key and any encrypted backup within 30 days. Uninstalling the app also removes on-device data.

Data export

You can export your own data as CSV or JSON from You → Import/Export at any time (GDPR Article 20 portability).

Children

Nini is intended for people aged 13 and older. Nini does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Legal bases and your rights

Nini processes health data only with your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a); Turkish KVKK Art. 6). Because data lives on your device, you exercise access, correction, portability and erasure rights directly in the app. For questions or requests write to techbaem@gmail.com.

Changes

When this policy changes, the effective date above changes and the app shows a notice. Previous versions are available on request.
